# Security Policy

We take security seriously at MarketView360. If you discover a security vulnerability, please report it responsibly.

# Reporting Guidelines
- Email security@marketview360.io with details
- Include steps to reproduce the issue
- Allow reasonable time for us to address before public disclosure
- Do not access or modify user data without permission

# Out of Scope
- Theoretical vulnerabilities without proof of concept
- Social engineering attacks
- Physical attacks
- Denial of service attacks

Thank you for helping keep MarketView360 secure!
